Disney Solitaire

I joined Superplay in 2024 and started with a Unity Technical Artist bootcamp, where the studio's pipelines and techniques were presented in depth, with plenty of exercises on what a Technical Artist at Superplay does day to day.

A month later I joined Disney Solitaire as a Unity Technical Artist, and that's where the magic began. Here are some of the features I worked on.

Crafting event · UI architecture · logic

Chef's Kitchen

The main UI is a scrollable area of dishes, with the characters and table rendered as 3D elements through a Render Texture. Each dish header has a descriptive image with a light sweep animation, dynamically set rewards and counters, and states for idle, selected, ready to cook and completed.

The Pantry combines vertical and horizontal layout groups, with its counter driven by our in-house visual scripting editor. Animations use Timelines and Playable Directors alongside Animators.

Timed event · layout · UI VFX

Chef's Kitchen: Daily Dish

Daily Dish adds a time-based event on top of the original Chef's Kitchen: a border and bonus rewards on a dish, shown in purple when idle and green when selected.

The challenge was getting a horizontal layout group and a content size fitter to scale and animate correctly for however many rewards the monetization team sets. It also introduced a new kind of timer, built with both code and our in-house visual scripting. Sparkles come from the UI particle system.

About

The bridge between art and tech. Some days, the team's peace keeper.

QADevArtAnimationProductMonetization

On Disney Solitaire I work hand in hand with QA, Dev, Art, Animation, Product and Monetization. At first that was overwhelming; over time it became second nature. My main job is to bridge the technical and the artistic side while understanding what Product and Monetization need.

I started on a small team of one Technical Artist, two QA, one Dev and one Product. As my skills grew I got to work with every team on the project and take part in many more features, which taught me a lot about teamwork, people management and Tech Art.

Working on an Agile timeline with short, tight deadlines, I learned to manage my time well and make technical decisions that keep deliverables to the highest standard: running smoothly and looking as whimsical as possible.

Beyond the technical skills, this project taught me to understand every job family and build the synergy between departments that gets each feature the best outcome.
